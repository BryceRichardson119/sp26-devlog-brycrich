ICEX 7 (Makeup) exposed a technical issue when I attempted to validate the duplicate-rating constraint. Specifically, sending two identical POST /jokes/7/ratings requests returned 201 Created both times instead of failing on the second request. This indicated that the system was not enforcing uniqueness at the database level. To diagnose the problem, I inspected the models/rating.py file and confirmed that no UniqueConstraint('user_id', 'joke_id') was defined. I also verified the behavior through repeated curl requests and observed no IntegrityError being raised in the logs. I resolved the issue by adding the missing UniqueConstraint to the model, generating and applying a new migration, and rerunning the test. After the fix, the first POST returned 201, and the second correctly triggered an IntegrityError, which I handled as a 409 Conflict response. This confirmed that the constraint was functioning as intended and aligned with the expected idempotency and data integrity requirements.
